Usha ma,

I am genuinely sorry that I didn’t ask you to come with me to the temple. I understand now that it wasn’t only about going somewhere—it was about feeling remembered, included, and wanted beside me. I should have thought of you and asked you. I didn’t, and I understand why that hurt you.

You are my Bangaram. When I pray, I want my actions outside the temple to carry the same care and respect. This time, my action did not show that. I won’t hide behind an excuse. I was wrong, and I am truly sorry.

I cannot undo that moment, but I can learn from it. From now on, I will communicate better and make sure you never have to wonder whether your presence matters to me. It does—more than I sometimes know how to express.

I made this little place not to pressure you to forgive me, but to tell you clearly and honestly that I understand my mistake and want to do better. Take all the time you need. My temple visit would have felt more complete with you beside me.

I’m really sorry, Usha ma.
